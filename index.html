Privacy Policy for Flashlight Tag

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Flashlight Tag is a local Wi-Fi multiplayer game. It does not create accounts, does not show ads, and does not sell in-app items.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Android app Flashlight Tag published on Google Play by Actinium Gaming Studio. Contact: use the email address shown on the Google Play listing, or open an issue on the public project repository.

What the game does

Players on the same local network (Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot) join a hosted match with a short code. The host device is also a player. There is no Flashlight Tag game server on the internet.

Information the app uses

On-device and on the local network only

  • A random join code derived from the host phone’s local network address so friends can type it and find the host on the same LAN.
  • Gameplay state for the current match (roles, movement, tags, keys, scores). That state is sent between the phones in the match, not to us.
  • Settings you choose in the app (for example volume), stored on that phone.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — so phones in the match can talk to each other on the local network. The app does not require a login or cloud account.

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or payment details. It does not use advertising identifiers.

Analytics, ads, and children

We do not run our own analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or profiling services in the game. The game is a 2–10 player local match. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not enter personal information in player names.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Match data stays among the phones in that match. Google Play may process install and review data under Google’s own policies when you use Play.

Retention

When the match ends or you close the app, the session is gone. We do not keep a server-side history of matches.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the copy linked from the Play Store listing.

Your choices

Uninstall the app to remove it and its on-device settings from that phone. You can also contact us using the Play listing email.
