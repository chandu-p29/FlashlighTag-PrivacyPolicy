Privacy Policy for Flashlight Tag

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Flashlight Tag is a multiplayer game for local Wi-Fi and optional online friend matches. It does not create a Flashlight Tag account, does not show ads, and does not sell in-app items.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Android app Flashlight Tag published on Google Play by Actinium Gaming Studio. Contact: use the email address shown on the Google Play listing, or open an issue on the public project repository.

What the game does

Players join a hosted match with a short code. The host device is also a player. There is no Flashlight Tag dedicated game server.

  • Local game: phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot talk to each other directly.
  • Online match: the host phone is still the game server. Epic Online Services is used only to find the lobby and carry match packets when friends are not on the same network. Players do not need an Epic account.

Information the app uses

On-device and in the current match

  • A join code. Local codes are derived from the host phone’s LAN address. Online codes are random lobby names used to find the host through Epic.
  • Gameplay state for the current match (roles, movement, tags, keys, scores). Local matches send that only between the phones. Online matches send it between the phones via Epic (direct P2P when possible, otherwise Epic’s relay).
  • Settings you choose in the app (for example volume), stored on that phone.
  • For online matches, Epic creates an anonymous device identifier so phones can connect. We do not receive that identifier.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — local matches on the LAN, and online matches through Epic. The app does not require a Flashlight Tag login.

The app does not collect your email, contacts, location, photos, or payment details. It does not use advertising identifiers. Player names stay in the current match.

Analytics, ads, and children

We do not run our own analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or profiling services in the game. The game is a 2–10 player match. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not enter personal information in player names.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Local match data stays among the phones in that match. Online match data is processed by Epic Games (Epic Online Services) so friends can connect; see Epic’s privacy policy. Google Play may process install and review data under Google’s own policies when you use Play.

Retention

When the match ends or you close the app, the session is gone. We do not keep a server-side history of matches.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the copy linked from the Play Store listing.

Your choices

Uninstall the app to remove it and its on-device settings from that phone. You can also contact us using the Play listing email.
